## Slide 2 — Why we are here

**We cannot protect, comply with, or optimise what we cannot see.**

- Hardware visibility was established last year (362k assets) — but Cloud, Software, AI and OT
  remain partially covered, and unmanaged assets weaken incident response, vulnerability management
  and compliance evidence at the same time.
- AI regulation is arriving with our AI readiness at **~30%** — registry, EU AI Act risk
  classification and continuous evidence are not yet operational.
- **The decision before you is not whether, but how far:** four scenarios, each fully costed,
  each with explicit outcomes and explicit trade-offs — slides 9–10.

*Speaker note: keep the room problem-first; the numbers have all been pre-validated in the
scenario modeller.*

## Slide 3 — What we already proved last year

- **362,000** IT enterprise assets made visible — the hardware foundation exists.
- **55%** improvement in asset trust for security use cases.
- **10M SEK** annual licence savings identified.
- **30%** reduction in incident identification time.
- **40%** improvement in ISO 27K compliance.

**Message:** whichever scenario you choose, it scales a capability that already pays.

## Slide 4 — The risk of standing still

Four enterprise risks if coverage is not extended (programme risk register):

| Risk | Severity | Business impact |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete enterprise asset visibility | **High** | Unmanaged/unidentified assets; weaker incident, vulnerability and compliance management; no enterprise single source of truth |
| Asset-intelligence value scale-up | **High** | Return on value already activated erodes; AI governance and regulatory readiness delayed |
| Data quality regression | Medium | Stale records return; audit readiness and control effectiveness degrade |
| Vendor / tool-owner / regional dependency | Medium | Onboarding of remaining asset classes stalls; fragmented processes persist |

**Message:** the cheapest scenario is not "do nothing" — that option carries the full unmanaged-risk
exposure we hold today.

## Slide 7 — How it is delivered: four functions (not four vendor contracts)

The programme is staffed by **function**, sourced across delivery partners and internal teams:

| Function | What it does | Investment (SEK) | Team size (FTE) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategic assurance & value governance** | Roadmap assurance, executive steering support, architecture and control validation, benefits challenge | 30.0M | 2.0 – 5.0 |
| **Data foundation & governance** | Asset data clean-up and enrichment (hardware, cloud, SAM inputs, AI registry), BAU governance | 8.4M | 3.0 – 6.0 ⚠️ (onsite 1–2 ⚠️ + offshore 2–4 ⚠️) |
| **Platform & tooling enablement** | Automated discovery/ingestion, reconciliation, CMDB integration, dashboards, incident/vulnerability context | 3.0M | 0.5 – 2.0 ⚠️ |
| **Internal ownership & adoption** (H&M) | Cyber security architecture leadership, business-unit stewardship, enterprise architecture alignment | — (internal) | internal capacity |

⚠️ = ceiling not yet confirmed in the resourcing model — **confirm before external circulation**.

- **Minimum team runs 2 workstreams in parallel**; the fuller scenarios need the upper bounds.
- Value attribution across these functions sums exactly to the 43.0M SEK directional value
  (8M assurance / 11M data / 12M platform / 12M internal adoption).
- Scenario choice directly sets the team size: smaller scenarios run at or near the minimums.

## Slide 9 — The four scenarios (the decision slide)

Cumulative slices of the roadmap, in delivery order. Funded workstreams are delivered whole —
a smaller scenario trades **breadth, not quality**.

| | **A. Minimum viable** | **B. Security first** | **C. Compliance ready** | **D. Full uplift** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Hardware | + AI + Cloud | + OT | + Software + New & Emerging |
| Investment | **8.6M SEK** | **25.9M SEK** | **31.1M SEK** | **41.4M SEK** |
| Directional value | 19.25M (2.23x) | 29.75M (1.15x) | 34.5M (1.11x) | 43.0M (1.04x) |
| Unmanaged tech risk reduction | 18% | 67% | **80%** | **80%** |
| Compliance readiness | 9% | 56% | **80%** | **80%** |
| Incident-response uplift | 12% | 20% | 27% | 30% |
| Financial benefit share of full plan | 45% | 69% | 80% | 100% |
| EU AI Act readiness | — (AI workstream not funded) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| What is **not** covered | Cloud, OT, Software/SAM, New & Emerging — 5 of 6 asset domains stay partially unmanaged | OT estate and SAM/licence savings deferred | SAM/licence optimisation and Data-thon value cycle deferred | — |

*Reading guide:* risk reduction and compliance readiness are delivered by Hardware + AI + Cloud +
OT — **Scenario C is the smallest scope that reaches both 80% targets**. The financial (SAM/Cloud)
benefit upside grows most between B → D.

## Slide 10 — The ask

1. **Select one of the four scenarios (A–D) as the funded 12-month scope.** Each is costed from the
   same rate card; none requires re-estimation after your decision.
2. Confirm the three ⚠️ FTE ceilings on slide 7 so resourcing can be locked for the chosen scope.
3. Endorse the internal ownership & adoption function (cyber architecture + business stewardship)
   as a named commitment — it carries 12M SEK of the programme's value at zero external cost, in
   every scenario.
4. Agree the value-governance cadence: quarterly benefit tracking via the Data-thon evidence cycle
   and the Strategic Value Framework metrics — so next year's decision is made on measured value,
   not estimates.
